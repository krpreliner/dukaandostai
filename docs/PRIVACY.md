# DukaanDost AI - Data Privacy & Synthetic Data Policy

## 1. Principles of Data Privacy

DukaanDost AI is designed with respect for the privacy of small retail shop owners and their neighbourhood customers.

1. **Store Owner Ownership:** All inventory, financial, and customer data entered into a store workspace belongs strictly to that store owner.
2. **No Data Aggregation or Reselling:** DukaanDost AI does not pool, train public AI models on, or monetize tenant retail data.
3. **Data Isolation:** Data belonging to one store workspace is cryptographically isolated and never accessible to another store workspace.

---

## 2. Synthetic Data Declaration

To facilitate comprehensive evaluation without jeopardizing real commercial entities:

- **Fictional Retail Entity:** **Sharma General Store**, located in **Ranchi, Jharkhand, India**, operated by **Sharma Ji**, is a fictional kirana store constructed solely for demonstration, testing, and academic evaluation.
- **Synthetic Records:** All 120 customer profiles, 30+ product SKUs, 320 transactions, and recurring store expenses are synthetic records generated by deterministic algorithms.
- **Simulated Outcomes:** Simulated improvements (e.g., closing the ₹2,000 income gap to achieve ₹10,000 monthly net income) are mathematical simulations produced by rule-based and agentic business logic. They are **not** real-world bank account deposits or guaranteed financial gains.

---

## 3. Customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Protection

- **Synthetic Phone Numbers:** All generated customer telephone numbers use fictional prefixes (e.g. `+91 98765 00001` through `+91 98765 00120`).
- **Sandboxed Messaging:** The messaging engine (`MessagingIntegration`) generates localized promotional drafts (Hindi & English). It **never transmits automated SMS or WhatsApp messages to real telecommunications networks** without explicit store owner carrier integration, recipient opt-in consent, and Indian TRAI / DLT regulatory compliance.
- **Tenancy Deletion:** Deleting a store workspace initiates an immediate cascading deletion of all associated customer records, purchase histories, and audit records.
